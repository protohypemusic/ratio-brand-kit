# Ratio files, type by type

Colors and fonts are defined in `SKILL.md`. This file says how to apply them.

## Word documents and PDFs (reports, memos, letters, proposals)

- Letter size, 1 in margins.
- Header: `assets/logo/ratio-logo-color.png` top left, about 1.6 in wide.
  A thin `#E2E8F0` line under the header across the full text width.
- Title: Lexend SemiBold 20 pt, Navy `#0F172A`. Optional subtitle or date
  under it in Source Sans 3 11 pt, Slate `#64748B`.
- Heading 1: Lexend SemiBold 14 pt, Navy, 18 pt space above.
- Heading 2: Lexend Medium 12 pt, Navy.
- Body: Source Sans 3 11 pt, Ink `#1E293B`, line spacing 1.2, 6 pt after
  each paragraph. Left aligned, never justified.
- Tables: header row Navy fill with white Source Sans 3 SemiBold text; body
  rows with `#E2E8F0` hairline borders; optional Paper `#F8FAFC` on alternate
  rows; numbers right aligned; a total row in SemiBold with a line above it.
- Callout box (one key figure or decision): Light blue `#DBEAFE` fill, Navy
  text, no border.
- Footer on every page, Source Sans 3 8 pt, Slate:
  `Ratio Accounting | ratio-accounting.com | 415-481-9008` left, page number
  right ("Page 2 of 4").
- A document about one client's finances: add "Prepared for [Client name],
  [Month Year]" under the title, and "Confidential" in the footer.

## Spreadsheets (Excel)

- First row (or a top block) with the client or report name in Lexend
  SemiBold 14 pt Navy, and the period/date under it in Slate.
- Column header row: Navy `#0F172A` fill, white bold Source Sans 3 11 pt,
  frozen so it stays visible when scrolling.
- Body: Source Sans 3 11 pt, Ink. Hairline `#E2E8F0` borders or Paper
  `#F8FAFC` banding, not both.
- Money: `#,##0.00` (or `#,##0` for summaries), negatives in parentheses.
  Percentages one decimal. Dates as `MMM d, yyyy`.
- Totals row in bold with a top border. Column widths set so nothing shows
  `####`.
- Name the sheet tabs in plain words ("Summary", "Detail", "2025"), never
  "Sheet1".
- No logo image inside the grid. If a cover sheet is wanted, put the color
  logo at the top of a separate "Summary" sheet.

## Slide decks

- 16:9, white backgrounds.
- Title slide and closing slide may use a Navy `#0F172A` background with
  `ratio-logo-white.png`. Every other slide is white with the color logo or
  the small triangle mark in a corner.
- Slide titles: Lexend SemiBold 28 to 32 pt, Navy. Body: Source Sans 3 16 to
  20 pt, Ink. One idea per slide; at most five bullets.
- Highlight the one number that matters in Blue `#2563EB`, large.
- Charts use the series order in `SKILL.md`, no 3D, no gradients, labels on
  the bars rather than a legend where possible.

## Emails

- Most client email is plain text written in Gmail. Follow `writing.md` and
  keep it short. No logo or images in the body.
- If an HTML email is needed (a designed announcement), match the portal's
  emails: light gray page `#F7F7F8`, a white card 600 px wide with 12 px
  rounded corners, `ratio-logo-color-small.png` centered at the top (max
  56 px tall), body text Ink, and buttons that are white with a 2 px Navy
  border, Navy text and 10 px rounded corners. No filled orange or blue
  buttons.

## Charts and visuals

- White background, light `#E2E8F0` gridlines or none, no chart borders.
- Lexend for the chart title, Source Sans 3 for labels.
- Series order: Blue, Navy, Slate, Sky (see `SKILL.md`). Use Positive and
  Negative only when color means good or bad.
- Start bar charts at zero. Label the period on every chart.
